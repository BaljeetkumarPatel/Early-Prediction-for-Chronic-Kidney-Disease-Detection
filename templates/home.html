What is CKD?

A progressive condition where kidneys lose filtering ability over time. Our AI detects early signs before symptoms appear.

⚠️

Risk Factors

Diabetes, hypertension, obesity, smoking, and genetics. Our system analyzes 50+ parameters to assess your risk profile.

🔬

Early Symptoms

Fatigue, swelling, urination changes, nausea. ML algorithms detect subtle patterns in your health data.

💧

Prevention

Stay hydrated, maintain a balanced diet, exercise regularly, and monitor blood sugar and pressure levels.

PREVENTION PROTOCOL

Evidence-based strategies for kidney health

💧

Hydration Optimization

Maintain 2-3 liters daily water intake. Proper hydration helps kidneys flush toxins and maintain optimal function.

🥗

Nutritional Balance

Low sodium, controlled protein, high fiber diet. Reduce processed foods and focus on whole grains, fruits, vegetables.

🏃

Regular Exercise

Engage in at least 30 minutes of moderate activity most days. Exercise improves circulation and overall kidney health.

🩸

Blood Pressure Control

Keep blood pressure within healthy limits through diet, exercise, and medication if needed. High BP damages kidneys.

🍬

Blood Sugar Management

Crucial for diabetics to prevent kidney damage. Monitor levels closely and adhere to treatment plans.

🚭

Avoid Smoking

Smoking severely impacts kidney function and accelerates damage. Quitting is a vital step for kidney health.

💊

Medication Awareness

Be cautious with NSAIDs and other nephrotoxic drugs. Consult your doctor about kidney-safe alternatives.

🗓️

Regular Check-ups

Annual physicals with kidney function tests are important, especially if you have risk factors.

Key Risk Factors

Understanding your risk factors is the first step toward prevention and early detection.

Diabetes

High blood sugar levels can damage kidney blood vessels.

High Blood Pressure

Hypertension is a leading cause of kidney damage.

Family History

Genetic predisposition increases CKD risk.

Age Factor

Risk increases with age, especially after 60.

Smoking

Damages blood vessels, reducing blood flow to the kidneys.

Obesity

Increases the risk of diabetes and high blood pressure.

Certain Medications

Long-term use of some drugs can harm kidney function.

Ethnicity

Certain ethnic groups have a higher predisposition.
